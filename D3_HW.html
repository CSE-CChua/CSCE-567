The scatter plot below shows the number of drivers and vehicles in the US for each year from 1960 through 2023. The red circles are for years when the number of drivers was larger than the number of vehicles, and blue circles are for years when the number of drivers was less than or equal to the number of vehicles.

The data used in this visualization is from the Department of Transportation and is available at data.gov.
